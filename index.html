FAQ

Common questions

What is Wanaka?+

Wanaka is an AI-assisted 3D game studio. Describe a game, play the first version, then keep directing the AI or edit the world yourself.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You describe what you want and the creation agent builds it with you — no programming experience required.

What kinds of games can I make?+

Racers, shooters, platformers, tower defense, exploration, puzzles, simulations, multiplayer arenas, city builders, and more.

Where can I play Wanaka games?+

In any modern browser — no download required; just open the link on any device.

How do I share what I've made?+

Publish in one click and share the link — anyone can open and play it in the browser.
